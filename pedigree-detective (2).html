Field Notes: Reading a Pedigree

Shapes
Square = male
Circle = female
Shading
Filled / colored = affected (shows the trait)
Unfilled = unaffected
Marriage / mating line
A horizontal line connecting two individuals means they are partners who had children together.
Offspring line
A vertical line dropping from a couple down to a horizontal "sibling line," with stems to each child, connects parents to their children.
Generations
Roman numerals (I, II, III...) on the left label each generation, oldest at the top. Individuals within a generation are numbered left to right (I-1, I-2...).
